Shroom 1.1.13

- Fixed an issue where the app did not fully quit during a macOS auto-update installation, which prevented ShipIt from taking over and replacing it.
- On quit, the app now shuts down the background WebSocket, serial-port reconnection, jqbed polling, the Python worker, and the local static server.
- Improved macOS auto-update stability, reducing cases where the app quits without installing.
