# DriverKit / CH340 implementation references

CH340 initialization and baud divisor calculation adapt the MIT-licensed implementation in [usb-serial-for-android, Ch34xSerialDriver.java](https://github.com/mik3y/usb-serial-for-android/blob/master/usbSerialForAndroid/src/main/java/com/hoho/android/usbserial/driver/Ch34xSerialDriver.java). The driver was not copied from the GPL Linux kernel. DriverKit lifecycle, matching and checked UserClient dispatch follow [Apple's Communicating Between a DriverKit Extension and a Client App sample](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/driverkit/communicating-between-a-driverkit-extension-and-a-client-app).
